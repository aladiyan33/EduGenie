EDUGENIE — EMPATHY MAP

SAYS
“I need a simple explanation.” “I want a quick answer.” “I need practice questions.” “I have too much study material.” “I do not know what to study next.”

THINKS
Learning should be clear and easy to follow. Time is limited. A single learning workspace is useful. Immediate feedback can improve practice.

DOES
Searches for explanations, reads notes, finds practice questions, summarizes material, and plans topics.

FEELS
Confused by difficult concepts, pressed for time, motivated by structure, satisfied by immediate feedback.

PAINS
Scattered resources, difficult terminology, manual quiz creation, lengthy material, unclear learning sequence.

GAINS
Clear explanations, focused answers, instant quiz feedback, condensed notes, structured learning recommendations.
