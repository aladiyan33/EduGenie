EDUGENIE — BRAINSTORMING & IDEA PRIORITIZATION

Project: EduGenie: Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

Brainstormed ideas
1. AI question answering for study doubts.
2. Simple concept explanation for difficult topics.
3. Automatic quiz generation from a topic.
4. Long-passage summarization for study material.
5. Beginner-to-advanced learning recommendations.
6. Future voice-based learning assistance.
7. Future multilingual learning support.
8. Future adaptive learning based on student progress.

Selected solution
EduGenie combines the five core learning capabilities in one lightweight web application: Q&A, concept explanation, summarization, quiz generation, and learning recommendations.

Prioritization
Core: Q&A, Explanation, Summarization, Quiz, Learning Recommendations.
Future: Voice, multilingual support, adaptive learning, gamification.
