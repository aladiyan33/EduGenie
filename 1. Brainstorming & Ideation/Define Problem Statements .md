EDUGENIE — DEFINE PROBLEM STATEMENTS

Consolidated problem statement
Students need a single, accessible learning assistant that can answer questions, simplify concepts, generate practice quizzes, summarize study material, and provide a structured learning path.

Problem statements
1. Students often need quick, understandable answers to study questions.
2. Complex academic concepts can be difficult to understand from textbook-style explanations alone.
3. Creating practice questions manually consumes study time.
4. Long study passages can be difficult to review quickly.
5. Students may not know what to learn next or how to progress from beginner to advanced level.
