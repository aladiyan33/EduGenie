EDUGENIE — DEFINE PROBLEM STATEMENTS

Consolidated problem statement
Students need a single, accessible learning assistant that can answer questions, simplify concepts, generate practice quizzes, summarize study material, and provide a structured learning path.

Problem statements
• Students often need quick, understandable answers to study questions.
• Complex academic concepts can be difficult to understand from textbook-style explanations alone.
• Creating practice questions manually consumes study time.
• Long study passages can be difficult to review quickly.
• Students may not know what to learn next or how to progress from beginner to advanced level.
